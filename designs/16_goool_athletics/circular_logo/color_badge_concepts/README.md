## Why these are concepts and not print masters

The design page carries its own warning: this direction **contradicts a
written artwork rule and adds print cost**. Knocking the mark out of a
filled disk means printing a large solid area rather than letters
alone, which is a different and more expensive print than the approved
badge.

Two of the four do at least use inks already approved elsewhere in the
line: forest is the crewneck ink (GA-CIRCLE-09), and navy-on-ivory is
the inverse of the approved Circular Badge Tee pairing
(GA-CIRCLE-08 on Comfort Colors C1717). Red is brand red `#C1121F`,
which the colour rules hold back for action rather than garment
graphics.

These are 1080 screen exports in any case. If a direction is chosen it
must be rebuilt as a 300 dpi master at actual print size, requoted with
Apliiq, and proofed before anything is ordered.
