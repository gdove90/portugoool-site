# Grok Imagine prompts, Goool avatars

## Grok settings
Model: Image. Quality 2.0. Aspect 2:3 Tall. Output count set to the minimum in the app. Do not scroll the results grid; scrolling generates more.

## Base style (append to every avatar prompt, unchanged)
Full-body studio photograph. Neutral seamless light-grey background, soft even three-point lighting, no shadows on the backdrop, camera at chest height, 85mm equivalent, subject centered, feet visible, arms relaxed at sides, neutral expression, no props, no jewelry, no hat, no logos. Wearing a plain fitted heather-grey crew-neck t-shirt, plain black athletic shorts to mid-thigh, white low-top sneakers, white ankle socks. Photorealistic, 4:5, no text, no watermark.

## Angle lines (use exactly one per run)
FRONT: Facing the camera directly.
BACK: Facing directly away from the camera, back fully visible.
LEFT: Full left profile, subject's left side to the camera.
RIGHT: Full right profile, subject's right side to the camera.

## Reference runs
Generate FRONT first. For BACK, LEFT and RIGHT, attach the saved front image as the reference and begin the prompt with: "Same man as the reference image, identical face, hair, build and clothing." Then the angle line, then the base style.
