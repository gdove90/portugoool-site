# Final image-generation prompt set

All nine images were generated separately with the built-in image workflow in the `ads-marketing` category. Every prompt requested a photorealistic 9:16 vertical master, crop-safe composition, premium football-culture art direction, no product substitution, no text, no logos, no club identifiers, and no watermark.

## P1 — Floodlit goal

Create a premium cinematic editorial photograph of an empty football goal under floodlights at night, viewed perfectly straight-on and centered from inside the penalty area. A thin layer of cold fog and visible moisture hangs in the air, suggesting the held breath immediately before a decisive goal. Anonymous community football pitch with no signage, teams, spectators, or identifiable landmarks. Exact visual symmetry, generous dark negative space, wet grass, fine fog, worn white net, cool desaturated color, and subtle red warmth only in distant haze. No clothing, people, ball, logos, text, or fantasy stadium.

## P2 — Chain-link pitch

Create a premium cinematic photograph looking through an anonymous black chain-link fence toward an empty community football pitch under floodlights at night. The fence is sharp in a small foreground plane while the goal and lights dissolve into elegant bokeh. Asymmetrical but calm, center-weighted, with generous dark space for optional copy. Cool white floodlights, deep shadows, wet atmosphere, and a restrained red point in the distance. No people, products, text, signage, logos, club colors, or identifiable venue.

## P3 — Empty stand

Create a cinematic editorial photograph of a single anonymous person seen only as a small dark silhouette, seated alone high in an otherwise empty concrete football stand, facing an unseen pitch at night. It should evoke the silent second before eruption, not sadness. Use repetitive rows, negative space, cool floodlight spill, off-white concrete highlights, and a subtle red edge reflection. Keep the face entirely unidentifiable and the clothing plain. No logos, text, signage, identifiable club, or crowd.

## V1 — Wet grass macro

Create an extreme macro editorial photograph of wet football grass at dusk, with individual blades and droplets resolving through very shallow focus. One faint painted white pitch line disappears into darkness. Low ground-level viewpoint, abstract but unmistakably football, with the upper frame reserved as soft dark negative space. Cold blue-black dusk, silver moisture, and one restrained distant red glow. No players, shoes, ball, logos, text, watermark, or synthetic CGI appearance.

## V2 — Backlit net

Create a cinematic close photograph of a white football goal net moving gently in night air, backlit by one hard floodlight so the wet fibers catch the light. The pitch behind falls into darkness and bokeh. Use diagonal folds for energy, a central safe zone, and large black negative space. Cool white backlight, deep shadows, humid mist, and tactile realism. No people, ball, text, logos, signage, club identifiers, or watermark.

## P4A — Bar table

Create a premium documentary-style photograph of an empty dark-wood table in a tasteful neighborhood football bar just before opening. Two simple empty glasses and two chairs imply a watch party waiting to happen; a distant television is only an abstract cool glow. Put the table in the lower third and leave clean center space for an approved product mockup inset. Use moody window light, black, warm wood, off-white, and one restrained red reflection. No alcohol labels, branded drinkware, scarves, jerseys, people, readable television image, logos, text, or watermark.

## P4B — Airport window

Create a cinematic travel photograph from the public departures side of an anonymous airport: a large terminal window overlooks a quiet runway at blue hour, with one empty black seat and an unbranded compact duffel nearby. Use a centered runway perspective and leave the middle clean for an approved hoodie mockup inset. Cool blue-grey dawn, black interior silhouettes, minimal warm runway lights, and one restrained red point. No people, airline marks, airport names, readable signs, flags, product logos, text, or watermark.

## P4C — City crosswalk

Create a premium streetwear editorial photograph of an anonymous clean city crosswalk at dusk immediately after rain, viewed straight-on from curb height. One distant pedestrian silhouette crosses but remains entirely unidentifiable. Use strong white crossing lines and centered negative space for an approved casual-tee mockup inset. Blue-black evening, wet pavement, subdued generic architecture, and a controlled red traffic-light reflection. No visible brand signs, readable plates, landmarks, clothing logos, text, watermark, or cyberpunk treatment.

## P4D — Pitch bench

Create a cinematic editorial photograph of an empty weathered pitch-side players bench beside an anonymous community football field at late dusk. A simple unbranded black water bottle rests beneath it; floodlights and a goal blur softly in the distance. Position the bench low and slightly off center with large clean middle and upper space for an approved performance-tee mockup inset. Use cool desaturated green, charcoal, wet surfaces, hard white floodlights, and one subtle red reflection. No people, balls, club crests, signs, logos, text, or watermark.
