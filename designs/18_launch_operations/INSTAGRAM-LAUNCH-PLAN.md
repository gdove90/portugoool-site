# Instagram-only launch — The Second It Goes In

Our proposed strategy: build recognition around one repeatable idea, then reveal the first sample-approved pieces. Start brand storytelling while the payment/account work is completed. Do not wait for every design to be ready; recommend an initial1–3product subset that has verified costs, samples and fulfillment. The owner chooses the subset. Do not sell unapproved products to meet a date.

This is a proposed compact sequence, not a promised launch date or an algorithm guarantee. All acquisition/publishing here is Instagram only: Reels, carousels, Stories and profile. No Facebook/TikTok/X crossposting, cold DMs, influencer outreach, paid ads or email campaigns. Existing on-site first-access capture can remain a destination; verify it before promising a signup succeeded.

## Prepare now

- Profile draft: “GOOOL Athletics • The Sound of Victory. Football-inspired apparel. First Capsule coming soon. First access ↓”. Confirm actual handle/account access rather than inventing one.
- Bio link: https://goool.shop/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=first_capsule&utm_content=bio. This opens the current public landing, not a gated product page. Do not include preview keys in public links.
- Pin three reviewed posts: brand story, first ready hero piece, how to buy/fit. Product and shopping posts wait for their own gates.
- Highlights: First Capsule, Fit, Details, FAQ. Populate only verified information.
- Use the existing 9:16 atmosphere files and 4:5 feed exports. Reframe editable text cards if key copy falls under Instagram UI; the old templates place some text near edges. Check the native preview on a phone.
- Existing D05 “FOUR PIECES” and D30 “door open” cards are not current launch-ready copy. Use INSTAGRAM-ASSET-SELECTION.json and newly reviewed captions.

## Sequence

## First Reel edit recipe

0–2s: wet-grass macro, “YOU KNOW THE SECOND.” 2–5s: backlit net, “BEFORE THE NOISE.” 5–8s: floodlit pitch, “THEN—GOOOL.” 8–11s: exact navigation wordmark on black, “THE FIRST CAPSULE / COMING SOON”. Use original or commercially cleared audio. Keep1main message and readable text, no rapid tiny captions. Label atmosphere as concept imagery where context might imply a real shoot/location; never present generated apparel as photographed samples.

## Measurement

Log results at 24h and 72h: views/reach, average watch time where available, saves, shares, profile visits, follows and link taps. Treat missing metrics as unavailable, not zero. Use Instagram's professional dashboard and its account-specific Best Practices guidance. [Meta's official explanation](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/). Reels watch-time metrics can help compare hooks; [Meta Reels insights](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/).

After the first 3 posts, keep the hook with better qualified profile visits/saves and watch time; change one variable in the next edit. UTM links do not automatically provide conversion attribution: verify an analytics destination first. Track actual orders only after launch; set no arbitrary revenue promise or ad spend.
