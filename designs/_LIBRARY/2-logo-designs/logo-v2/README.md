## How it differs from the primary

- Heavier letters with tighter spacing and smaller counters.
- A flatter diagonal cut through each O.
- A bolder underline set closer to the letters, with a much longer Match Red centre bar. All three bars have slanted ends.
- The patch adds ATHLETICS in wide-spaced caps under the underline.

## Status: concept raster, not production artwork

It came from the ChatGPT kitwear session as image 01, "Wordmark + proposed sewn label", captioned by ChatGPT as "Reconstructed artwork; not original vector masters". Before any print, embroidery or factory use:

1. **Redraw it as vector.** The wordmark is 756 px wide, about 76 ppi at a 10 in print. The redraw should also make the three O's identical.
2. **Use Match Red #C1121F.** The raster's red measures about #C41021.
3. **Check lettering at actual size** under the production lettering hold (`designs/19_colorway-and-back-print-review/README.md`).

Proposed use rule, for the owner to confirm: one logo per product. Never put the primary and V2 on the same item.
