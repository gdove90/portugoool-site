06 / PLACEMENT & SIZE GRADING
Measure the ink, not the canvas
Measurement convention
Lay the garment flat without stretching. Use the bottom seam of the rib collar at center front or center back
as the vertical origin. Measure straight down the body centerline to a horizontal line through the highest
visible printed point. For the left-chest graphic, use the same front origin and its specified horizontal center
offset. Do not measure from the top of the rib or the shoulder seam.
Center-front and center-back graphics align to the garment body centerline. All widths cover the whole
visible lockup, including any underline or flanking rules. Keep the artwork aspect ratio locked. Height is
recorded after artwork reconstruction; never stretch letters to fill an arbitrary box.

Geometry
Measure print width, height and collar offsets with a ruler. Verify centerline / left-chest

Print finish
No lifting edges, unwanted cracks, pinholes, exposed underbase halo, adhesive residue,

Fine type

Color / feel

Wash review
Proposed owner test: five wash/dry cycles according to the supplied care instructions.
Photograph before/after; check lifting, cracking, color loss and distortion. Record chest/body
dimensions before and after; compare with the supplier's declared shrinkage allowance.
Fulfillment
Check folded presentation, label information, packing slip accuracy and packaging. Send
care instructions for the chosen blank/process.
Photos and records
Provide full front/back photos on a flat surface, detail crops of every print, collar/label photos and ruler
photos of placements. Use neutral lighting with no filters. Record sample date, order ID, blank lot if
available and artwork revision. Keep one approved physical reference or agree an alternative standard.
When a sample fails
Document the issue against the approved proof and agree the correction/re-sample plan before fulfillment
is enabled. Ask the supplier to state its remedy and reprint policy in the quote; this packet does not
presume acceptance of commercial terms.
