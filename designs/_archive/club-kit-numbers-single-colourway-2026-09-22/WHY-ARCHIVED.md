# Club kit squad numbers, single colourway

Archived 2026-09-23.

These are the 25 back numbers as first delivered, one colourway, named
`2g-back-number-NN.png`. They were replaced the same week by a redrawn set
in two colourways, now at
`designs/_LIBRARY/1-PRINT-FILES/club-kit-2a-2l/numbers/ink/` and
`.../numbers/white/`.

The replacement is not a rename. Every one of the 25 differs byte for byte
from the file it replaces, and each is substantially larger, so the numerals
were redrawn rather than re-exported. The second colourway is the point: a
kit needs dark numerals for light shirts and white for dark ones, and the
original set only ever covered one of those cases.

Kept rather than deleted because these files may already have gone to a
supplier. Do not upload them. Use the two-colourway set.
