# Supplier guidance checked for revision 2

[ST720 construction](https://www.apliiq.com/customize/mens/tshirts/Sustainable-Athletic-Tee): Supplier lists recycled polyester, set-in sleeves and transfer printing. Static print-area table does not expose usable dimensions; verify active customizer.

[AS Colour 5150](https://www.apliiq.com/customize/mens/sweatshirts/Made-Crew): Supplier lists relaxed fit, French terry, drop shoulders and inset sleeves. Exact heather composition and color availability still require variant verification.

[Woven label artwork](https://help.apliiq.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-prepare-artwork-for-clothing-tags-and-woven-labels): Recommends 300dpi, accepts 150dpi and SVG; max 4 square inches, up to five colors, 1mm details, 9-10pt text guidance, 1/8in sewing margin. One-inch tag detail needs review.

[Full-color printed label artwork](https://help.apliiq.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-set-up-artwork-for-printed-labels): PNG recommended; 150dpi minimum, 300dpi recommended; up to 2x2in; continuous background shape; unlimited colors.

[Color-capable label service](https://www.apliiq.com/private-label/printed-labels-pressed-on-demand): Printed labels are DTF supplies stored and pressed on demand. This service exists, but inside-neck and exact blank/placement suitability are not established by this page.

[Label placement](https://help.apliiq.com/portal/en/kb/articles/placement-options-for-woven-labels): Placement guide covers woven and heat-transfer labels across tees, hats and hoodies. Confirm the individual placement in the account; category-level guide is not blanket compatibility.
