## How to execute this in Higgsfield

Do not paste the entire 30-second script into a single video-generation prompt and expect consistent results. Use the master brief as your creative reference, then generate the seven scenes as separate clips.

**Step 1 — Prepare your reference assets.** Upload your approved GOOOL Athletics shirt images, the official logo, and any existing product-on-model photography. Generate or choose a reference image of the actor standing in the bedroom wearing the real shirt. Reuse this image as the visual reference for subsequent scenes. Do not rely on a text prompt alone to reproduce your apparel design accurately.

**Step 2 — Generate the video clips.** Use Higgsfield's image-to-video workflow where available. For each scene, start with a reference frame and enter the corresponding scene-specific prompt from the master brief. Generate the bedroom scenes using the same character reference. Use a shirt-covered frame to connect the bedroom and stadium shots. Keep the goal sequence as several short shots if the model struggles to preserve realistic football movement.

**Step 3 — Add commentary and sound.** Assemble the footage in a video editor and add stadium ambience, crowd reactions, football sound effects and the commentator's sustained goal call. Align the commentator's scream precisely with the ball hitting the net. The sudden silence when the actor returns to his bedroom is an essential part of the story.

**Step 4 — Build the final title sequence.** Create the slogan, Core Collection launch announcement and official logo reveal as separate graphics. Composite the exact text and logo in the editing stage rather than asking AI video generation to reproduce the brand lettering. Apply the television-static effect only after the official GOOOL Athletics logo appears.

**Step 5 — Export your campaign versions.** Export the complete 30-second commercial, then create a shorter 15-second version using the same footage for paid advertising. Keep the shirt transformation, goal, return to reality, Core Collection announcement and logo reveal in both versions.

**One final creative detail:** The strongest transition in the entire commercial is the shirt covering the camera in the bedroom and revealing the stadium when it clears the lens. Prioritize getting that shot right. It makes the product itself responsible for the transformation and visually connects the feeling of scoring with wearing GOOOL Athletics.
