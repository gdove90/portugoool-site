transcribed.

**This is a different business from the retail catalog.** These put GOOOL
Athletics on someone else's kit, for local and youth sides. The owner
confirmed on 2026-09-23 that it is an intended expansion.

## What this line needs that retail does not

- **It is probably marketing spend, not revenue.** GOOOL pays to appear on
  a club's kit. Selling kit TO clubs is a third model again, with
  approvals, sizing runs and delivery dates.
- **Bulk is the one place Apliiq's pricing works.** Retail runs on dropship
  ($17.04 a tee, $29.88 a cap). At 144 units those are $15.82 and $17.11.
  Team orders are the only volume that reaches those tiers.
- **No youth sizes exist.** Every catalog product is S-XXL adult.
- **Legal is heavier.** Leagues cap sponsor dimensions, a club's crest is
  the club's mark and needs written permission, and photographing youth
  teams means minors and parental consent.
