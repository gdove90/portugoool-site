launch decision record. Nothing here blocks production. Three items need
the owner's word before the end cards are cut.

1. **Collection name mismatch.** The brief says "THE CORE COLLECTION".
   The site, the launch folder and the decision record all say
   **Core Capsule** (`CORE-CAPSULE-LAUNCH-2026-09-25/`,

   and the shop must say the same thing. Recommendation: end card reads
   "THE CORE CAPSULE" unless the owner renames the collection on the
   site first.

   feeling. / Wear the feeling." is new copy. The mark-locked lines are
   "The Sound of Victory." and "Made for the Moment." (GOOOL) and "The
   Language of the Game." (GOL). The new line does not cross those marks
   and can run as campaign copy. It is not a tagline substitute and must
   not replace "The Sound of Victory." anywhere on the site or product.
3. **Dash in the launch card.** "FIRST CAPSULE — NOW LIVE" uses an em
   dash. House copy standard is the middle dot: **FIRST CAPSULE · NOW
   LIVE**. Apply at the compositing stage; the brief text is left as
   received.
