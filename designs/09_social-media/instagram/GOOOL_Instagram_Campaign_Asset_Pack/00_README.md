### Usage rules

1. Use the exact crop folder for the intended placement. Do not stretch an image.
2. Add campaign copy in white or off-white; use `#C61322` only as the accent rule.
3. Keep all text inside Instagram safe areas. For Stories/Reels, keep critical copy away from the top 250 px and bottom 320 px.
4. Do not add club crests, player likenesses, broadcast stills, or borrowed music.
5. These are atmosphere images, not documentary claims. Do not geotag them as a specific stadium, bar, airport, or city.
6. Product composites must use the approved GOOOL mockups unchanged. Real-garment claims and on-body images wait for approved physical samples.

### Image-generation method

Generated with the built-in image workflow using nine separate production prompts in the `ads-marketing` category. The shared direction was photorealistic premium football-culture editorial imagery, 9:16 vertical composition, deep black and cool neutral color, restrained red ambient accents, crop-safe negative space, anonymous locations, and no text, logos, teams, signage, products, or watermarks.
