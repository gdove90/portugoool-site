# Instagram posting times (Eastern Time)

Owner's grid, saved 2026-09-30, verbatim. Sports apparel sits between
fitness (pre-gym / after-work) and fashion retail (lunch browse + evening
shop). Times are exact slots to put on the timer. Do not post feed and
Reel in the same hour.

**Daily stack**
- Story: morning (habit / commute / pre-training)
- Feed post: mid-day or early evening (look, product, carousel)
- Reel (Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat only): later window for reach

### Copy-paste timer list
- **Mon:** Story 6:30 AM · Feed 12:15 PM · Reel 7:00 PM
- **Tue:** Story 6:30 AM · Feed 5:00 PM
- **Wed:** Story 7:45 AM · Feed 12:00 PM · Reel 6:00 PM
- **Thu:** Story 6:30 AM · Feed 9:00 AM
- **Fri:** Story 6:30 AM · Feed 12:15 PM · Reel 3:00 PM
- **Sat:** Story 8:30 AM · Feed 11:00 AM · Reel 4:30 PM
- **Sun:** Story 8:30 AM · Feed 7:00 PM

That is **7 stories, 7 feed posts, 4 Reels** every week.

**Story extras that work (optional, not required):** a second Story at
**8:15 PM** on Tue / Thu / Sun only. Keep it light (poll, kit close-up,
reply sticker). Do not add a second Story on Reel days or you crowd the
same audience.

**What to put where**
- Morning Stories: locker, sock detail, "today's session," raw / behind-the-brand
- Feed: product stills, lookbooks, carousels, testimonials
- Reels: movement, strike, on-body, 9:16 commercial cuts

After 2 to 3 weeks, open Instagram Insights, Followers, Most active times
and shift any slot that is consistently 30 to 60 minutes off the real
audience. These times are the right starting grid for a US sports-apparel
account.

## How this folder feeds the grid

- `../feed/` holds feed stills (`_1080x1350`) and their 2:3 sources.
- `../stories/` holds 9:16 story cuts, usually re-cropped from a feed source.
- `../reels/` holds 9:16 reels and their cover frames.
- Every asset passes the checklist in `../README.md` before it is scheduled.
