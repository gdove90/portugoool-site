## Blocking checks before either enters the catalog

1. Blank: ST720 family is the candidate (existing Performance Badge
   Tee precedent). Verify current Apliiq stock, colors, size range,
   print areas and decoration compatibility - requires the Apliiq
   login session.
2. Artwork: production masters must be prepared from the original
   artwork sources, never cropped from these renders. The Modern Sport
   masters (GA-01-F/B) still carry rough extracted edges per QA-NOTES
   and need cleanup plus a print proof first.
3. Pricing: cost basis from real Apliiq garment + DTF + fulfillment
   quotes; proposal against the $48 Performance Badge Tee comparable.
   No invented prices.
4. No rear or model imagery exists for either concept; a full
   standard gallery needs front/back product and model sets.
5. Owner approval of both designs and names.

Do not infer technical performance properties from the renders; all
fabric claims wait for the verified blank specification.
