# 10 · Email

Email designs per flow: `welcome/` (list signup), `launch/` (drop announcements), `order/` (confirmation/shipping), `abandoned-cart/`.

**`production-emails/` holds the emails that actually exist today** — faithful records of the Mailchimp drafts (Mailchimp is the live sending system as of 2026-09-14; the Resend plan is superseded). `mockups_email-experience/` holds the concept boards for the wider email experience.
