## Print verification and limits

Official reference, checked 2026-09-25: https://help.apliiq.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-prepare-artwork-for-transfer-printing

Apliiq's transfer-print guidance calls for font strokes at least 2 mm thick, artwork prepared at 300 dpi at final size, and fully opaque printed pixels. Sharp points and isolated fine details can be difficult to transfer.

The current production files were independently measured without modification. See `print-audit.json` for per-letter values and SHA-256 hashes. These are approximate raster medial-skeleton thickness measurements with a padded glyph boundary; they are not a supplier proof or exact vector stroke measurement. This method is slightly more conservative than the earlier unpadded measurement in package 26.

| Production file | Recorded width | Native proportional height | Resolution at that size | Minimum ATHLETICS stroke measured |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GA-01-F_v3_3300px.png | 11 in / 279.4 mm | 3.72 in / 94.488 mm | 300 ppi | 2.2013 mm |
| GA-01-B_v3_1950px_600ppi.png | 3.25 in / 82.55 mm | 1.138333 in / 28.9133 mm | 600 ppi | 2.0320 mm |

Both masters contain alpha values 0 and 255 only. The ATHLETICS lettering clears 2 mm in this raster measurement **when the masters retain their native aspect ratio at these widths**. The back has only about 0.03 mm measured margin. Do not shrink it or use the 975 px / 300 ppi derivative as a substitute for the linked 1950 px master.

**An outstanding production-scale issue must be checked in the supplier proof.** Package 26 records the old saved back placement as 3.25 × 1.11 in, while the current high-resolution file's native aspect ratio at 3.25 in is 3.25 × 1.138333 in. If Apliiq compresses the file vertically to the old height, the conservative thickness bound becomes about 1.9814 mm. Preserve the high-resolution file's proportions; do not describe the back as unconditionally print-approved until Apliiq confirms how the linked high-resolution art is sized. The front's small height difference does not create this issue.

The GOOOL wordmark also contains pointed cut terminals below 2 mm at their tips; these are distinct from the measured ATHLETICS strokes and need supplier proof review. Smooth website renders cannot certify their physical transfer quality. No sample, transfer adhesion, or wash test was performed.

The Apliiq design IDs (6112037 Black and 6113361 True Royal), print widths, and four high-resolution upload activity entries are from the existing local package-26 audit. No authenticated Apliiq tab was available in this session, so those account records were not independently rechecked live. Supplier files, placements, SKUs and fulfillment settings were not changed by this cleanup.
