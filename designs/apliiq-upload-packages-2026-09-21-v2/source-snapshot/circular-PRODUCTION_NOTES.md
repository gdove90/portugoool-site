## Artwork

The letters themselves form a circle. No enclosing ring, solid disk, crest shield, or extra wording. GOOOL uses three Os. The badge is placement only. Single-color front decoration; no back print added by this handoff. Existing garment decoration direction is DTF, subject to verified current project instructions and Apliiq requirements. Embroidery would need a separate proof/decision, especially for the small ATHLETICS text.

Physical dimensions are starting targets, not inferred measurements from these mockups. Large-center concepts target 7 inches on tees and 6 inches on fleece; small-center 2.5 inches and badge 3 inches. Supplier placement templates and garment-size checks govern final settings.

Only a raster logo reference is included. No vector, digitized embroidery, or production print master is certified by this packet. Obtain a matching editable master from the project if available, or prepare one faithfully and verify all letters/spacing. Inspect transparency and edges; do not use the preview background as part of the print.
