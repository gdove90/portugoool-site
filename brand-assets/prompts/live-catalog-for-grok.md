## How to use this with Grok Imagine

1. Upload the product image named under each colorway as the garment reference. Grok will not reproduce the GOOOL marks accurately; keep them small and implied in the prompt, and overlay the real marks afterwards in Claude Design.
2. Put one of the four avatars in every shot with a person: Greg, Jeff, Alex or Andrew. Paste the character card from `brand-assets/avatars/PROMPTS.md` verbatim and attach that avatar's front view. Never invent a fifth person.
3. Reels are 9:16. Product-only shots are 2:3. Feed crops are 4:5.
4. Copy rules for anything with words on screen or in the caption: the line is WEAR THE FEELING. Say soccer or futbol, never football. Never show a price. Name only GOOOL Athletics, never a supplier or blank brand. No crests, no federation or competition marks, no other logos, no player likenesses.
