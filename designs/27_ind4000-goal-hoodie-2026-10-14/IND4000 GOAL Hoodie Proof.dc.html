Blank
Independent Trading Co IND4000 Heavyweight Hooded Pullover, 10 oz (via Apliiq). Colours: Black, Classic Navy, Bone, Grey Heather. Confirm the exact navy name in the Apliiq designer.
Research
Left chest is still the strongest placement on heavyweight fleece. Current direction keeps the front minimal and puts the brand story on the back. Champion's Reverse Weave puts its C logo on the left sleeve near the cuff, and Nike Tech Fleece puts the Swoosh on the left sleeve. Heritage follows that sleeve tradition.
Sizes
S, M, L, XL, XXL. Same files and positions on every size. S chest is 21 in flat.
Method
DTF transfer, all placements.
Apliiq limits
Transfer print maximums (Apliiq IND4000 page, 14 Oct 2026): front 13.5 × 15 in, back 15 × 19 in, each sleeve 3.5 × 19 in. Every placement fits: left chest GA 3.50 × 3.15, big stack 9.00 × 7.45, back wordmark 8.00 × 2.44, sleeve GA 3.00 × 2.70 (0.25 in spare each side of the 3.5 in sleeve width).
Measured from
Front: hood seam at centre front. Back: back hood seam at centre back. Left chest is the wearer's left.
Ink
Black: White #FFFFFF + Red #E51226. Classic Navy: Cream #F9F1DF, one colour. Bone and Grey Heather: Black #080808 + Red #E51226. The red A is softer on Grey Heather (about 2.3:1) than on Bone (2.8:1); the black lettering is strong on both.
Measured detail
GA at 3.50 in: smallest gap 3.78 mm. Back wordmark at 8.00 in: smallest gap 2.65 mm (same letter scale as the stack at 8.00 in). Stack at 9.00 in: smallest gap 2.42 mm. The 1.8–1.9 mm stroke readings are the pointed GA tips. The stack fails below 7.45 in wide (gaps in ATHLETICS drop under 2 mm), so 8.00 in is the smallest back width.
Clearances (S)
Big front: art ends 10.45 in below the CF hood seam; pocket top estimated at 15.00 in, 4.55 in clear. Back: hood lying flat estimated to end 11.80 in below the back hood seam; upper-back art sits 2.00–4.44 in below the back hood seam, under the hood when it is down. The below-hood option (12.25–14.69 in) is in Tweaks. Both use estimated seam positions; measure them on the Apliiq template.
