# 24 shirt spec sheets + 9 lockup colourways

Intake 2026-09-23 from Downloads (`goool (1).zip`, `goool.zip`, and the
loose `goool-athletics-lockup-white.png`). Filed as received. Nothing here
is approved, on the site, or uploaded to a supplier.

## What arrived

**`spec-sheets/` - 24 sheets in two series that are NOT the same business.**

- **1a-1l - retail.** Shirts GOOOL sells. Signature Chest, Three Dashes,
  Sound of Victory, Sleeve Hit, Six Os, What's Your GOOOL, Terrace Chant,
  Stoppage Time, Made for the Moment, One Word, We Don't Whisper, Gold
  Standard.
- **2a-2l - club kit sponsorship.** A different product entirely: GOOOL
  Athletics as the *sponsor* on someone else's kit, "for sponsoring local
  and youth sides". Crest Shield, Training Round, Chest Sponsor, Partner
  Lockup, Sleeve Patch, Dash Sash, Training Number, Staff Tab, Tonal,
  Pre-Match, Royal Training, Yoke Band.

That second series is a B2B line with different economics, different
minimums and different legal exposure. It should not be planned as if it
were twelve more retail SKUs.

**`lockups/` - 9 colourways.** all-gold, gold-standard, ink, ink-on-red,
mono-white, red-word, signature, terrace-red, white. See
`lockups-contact-sheet.png`. Note `signature` and `white` are close to
identical on screen; confirm they are actually different files before both
enter the brand library.

## Assessment against Apliiq - READ BEFORE PLANNING ANY OF THESE

**Two of the two sheets read in detail contain specs Apliiq cannot
execute.** Only 1a and 2c were opened; the remaining 22 have NOT been
checked and are likely to carry the same faults.

| sheet | spec as written | problem |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | "Comfort Colors 4810GD" | 4810GD is **Bella+Canvas**, not Comfort Colors. Comfort Colors is C1717. Two blanks conflated into one line. |
| 1a | "DTG back" | Apliiq does **not** offer DTG. Its methods are transfer print (DTF) and embroidery. |
| 2c | "Sublimation (all-white blank)" | Apliiq does **not** offer sublimation either. |

Apliiq's real decoration options, confirmed 2026-09-22 in the customizer:
**transfer print (DTF)** and **embroidery**. Nothing else. Any sheet
calling for DTG, sublimation, screen print or puff-as-a-method needs its
method rewritten or a second supplier.

Also unchecked across all 24: whether the stated print sizes clear the
limits that actually bind. For embroidery those are 2mm minimum detail and
1/4in minimum letter height - see
`../23_cap-embroidery-2026-09-22/APLIIQ-OTTO-31069-SPEC.md`. 1a's back
print is 2.5 x 0.3in, which is 7.6mm tall type and needs measuring before
anyone commits to it.

## What these sheets get right

The format is genuinely good and worth keeping: blank, method, placement
in inches from named datums (HPS, centre front, hem), and hex values for
every ink. That is a real tech pack, not a mood board. The faults above
are supplier-matching errors, not sloppiness.

## Next step

Read the remaining 22 sheets and build a table of blank / method / size
per design, marked against what Apliiq can actually run. Until that exists,
treat the set as a design direction rather than a production queue.
