# GOAL OPERATING KNOWLEDGE (shared continuous-learning record)

Owner decision 2026-10-28. One shared record for every Claude-side GOAL agent, scheduled or on demand. Agents read it before working and append to it afterwards. It holds validated lessons and clearly labelled hypotheses, never raw reports. The experiment record is `GOAL-EXPERIMENT-LEDGER.md` beside this file; per-post performance numbers stay in `marketing/daily-brief/content-ledger.md`.

## WHO READS, WHO WRITES
- Performance & Consumer Intelligence (01): reads; writes validated social-performance conclusions after each run.
- AEO, SEO & Ecommerce (03): reads and applies relevant validated findings; writes search, ecommerce and site-conversion lessons.
- Creative QA & Second-Opinion Specialist (04, on demand): reads before working; writes creative-execution lessons afterwards.
- Higgsfield Video Specialist (05, on demand): reads before working; writes video-execution lessons afterwards.
- Marketing Director strategy (02, on demand): reads before working.
Nobody rediscovers what is already recorded here. Nobody deletes another agent's entry; a superseded entry is marked INVALIDATED with a pointer to the newer one.

## EVIDENCE CLASSES (use exactly one per entry)
- OBSERVED: directly inspected evidence (a metric returned by Metricool, a page fetched, a file downloaded and inspected).
- SUPPORTED INFERENCE: a reasoned conclusion supported by identified evidence.
- EXPERIMENT: a hypothesis to test (record it in the experiment ledger too).
- NO DATA: insufficient evidence.
- REJECTED: explicitly rejected by Gary.
- APPROVED PREFERENCE: explicitly approved by Gary.
- INVALIDATED: a previously accepted belief contradicted by newer, stronger evidence.
Anything received from another agent and not confirmed from the authoritative source is RELAYED / UNVERIFIED and is not entered as OBSERVED.

## CONFIDENCE
LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH, stated on every entry. One result on a tiny base is LOW. A result repeated across products or weeks with a control rises to MEDIUM. Only repeated, controlled, consistent evidence reaches HIGH. Never promote one result into a permanent rule.

## OWNER FEEDBACK
Gary's explicit approvals, corrections and rejections carry high weight for GOAL taste and operating preferences. Record the underlying principle, not only the individual asset ("owner rejected X because Y"). Learned behaviour never overrides a FINAL_LOCKED rule, a standing owner rule or the owner-approval rule unless Gary explicitly changes that rule.

## PERFORMANCE LEARNING WINDOWS
Where actual data exists, review the 24-hour signal, the 72-hour signal and a 7-day comparison where useful. A snapshot that was not actually captured at that age does not exist: write DATA NOT AVAILABLE, never backfill.

## LEARNING BOUNDARY
Agents may learn and evolve: tactics, content patterns, creative execution, testing approaches, search opportunities, workflow efficiency, evidence-backed audience insights.
Agents may NOT change on their own: V1.3 branding, locked logos and artwork, pricing, product facts, company positioning, owner-approval requirements, legal restrictions, material company commitments. Those change only by Gary's explicit approval, and an entry may at most recommend that he consider it.

## STALE KNOWLEDGE CONTROL
A historical file never regains authority by being read. Old GOOOL naming, old slogans, stale prices, old colorways, old product catalogs, historical fixed model counts, superseded logos, old supplier assumptions and stale social-platform guidance are reference material only where verified current today against the live catalog, the current Drive library and current owner instructions. When a historical source conflicts with a current one, the current one wins and the entry says which source lost.

## NO FAKE LEARNING
If a run produced nothing material, the agent writes nothing here and reports NO MATERIAL NEW LEARNING. Lessons are never manufactured to fill a slot.

## ENTRY FORMAT (append under ENTRIES, newest last)
DATE: YYYY-MM-DD
SOURCE: agent and report file, or the owner
OBSERVATION: what was seen, in one or two sentences, with the numbers that matter
CLASSIFICATION: one evidence class
CONFIDENCE: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
IMPLICATION: what it means for GOAL
RECOMMENDED FUTURE ACTION: the next concrete step, always for OWNER REVIEW where it touches anything public or material
