# GOOOL men's studio gallery standard

Approved direction: studio fits without outdoor or action shots. Apply the same gallery sequence to every included product and exact color variant.

1. Front garment view, retaining the existing catalog cover.
2. Rear garment view, showing the complete garment.
3. Studio fit on the product's assigned model.
4. Logo detail.
5. Fabric or construction detail.

Use a soft white seamless backdrop, neutral white balance, diffused lighting and natural skin texture. Keep framing consistent within tees, hoodies and caps. Faces can look at the camera or turn naturally; photographic cohesion does not require identical gaze or expression. Keep one model identity per product across its colors.

## Artwork and placement

The existing product's supplier render is the authority for garment decoration. Keep its existing artwork, not a substituted website strike mark. Use the garment centerline, collar seam, torso width and print bounds to check centering, scale and the gap below the neckline. Print must follow the cloth's curvature and folds. Preserve the three Os in GOOOL, open corner gaps, underline segments and color-specific ink colors. Caps retain the natural crown and black visor.

The generated fit and detail images are visualizations, not physical sample photographs or manufacturing placement proofs. Their gallery captions disclose this. Supplier front covers remain the catalog and cart images.

## Coverage and provenance

The manifest maps eight existing products and eighteen existing colors by product ID, slug and exact variant name. It adds 57 WebP assets. Existing covers and rear views remain, except Matchday's prior rear detail crop is replaced by a complete rear visualization. Each color resolves to five gallery positions, for 90 positions total.

The Touchline Cap is excluded from this photography work. Its catalog status is unchanged.

`mens-studio-v3.json` is the exact gallery manifest. The asset inventory records generated PNG original names, native dimensions and original/export SHA-256 checksums. WebP exports preserve native dimensions and aspect ratios. The gallery uses the existing contained image presentation.

Photography references examined: Nike N.A.C. Men's T-Shirt and Vuori Strato Tech Tee. Their published galleries informed natural variation in gaze and framing. No competitor faces, photographs or garments are distributed in this asset release.
