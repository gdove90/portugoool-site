## Changes from v2 and why

- Opening moved from a night bedroom to a daytime couch: no undressing
  (Higgsfield's filter blocked every shirt-over-head take), and the Casual
  Wordmark Tee is shown for four seconds before the performance tee.
- The transport is a dolly into the TV picture rather than a shirt over
  the lens. The costume change happens inside the screen, so it reads as
  "he becomes the player", not a continuity error.
- The dribble is specified move by move (behind-camera run, left lunge
  without touching the ball, right drag with the outside of the boot,
  immediate right-footed strike, top-right corner) across three clips so
  each can be nailed separately.
- New celebration beats: a 360-degree orbit that lands on the front of
  the black tee, and the corner-flag moment.
- Every prompt now carries Subject, Action, Environment, Lighting, Camera
  angle, per the owner's five-part standard.
