# GOOOL preliminary US trademark review

Research date: September 12, 2026. Read-only research; no application submitted or fees paid.

## Scope and conclusion

The intended brand is GOOOL. Both owner-approved slogans are The Sound of Victory and Made for the Moment. Intended launch goods are T-shirts, hooded sweatshirts, and caps. Rhode Island is the operating state.

This is a preliminary screening, not a comprehensive clearance opinion. GOOOL is not cleared for use or filing by this review. Similar live clothing marks require assessment. State entity-name availability does not establish trademark availability.

Web research also surfaced historical German goool.de sportswear use. Current rights, US use, ownership and international registrations were not established. Intervision's own page confirms GOOOL! food-brand use: https://www.ivfoods.com/products/goool.

## Proposed filing sequence, conditional on clearance

1. Obtain a trademark attorney's assessment of GOL, GOAL and the Genesco slogan application, including full records, designs, goods, priority, marketplace use and relevant unregistered rights.
2. Establish the actual owner. If a newly formed LLC will own the brand, form it before naming it as applicant. Do not name a proposed/nonexistent LLC.
3. If supported by clearance, prioritize GOOOL as a standard-character mark for the intended Class 025 goods. Validate identification wording in the USPTO ID Manual. Do not add speculative goods.
4. If qualifying use has not begun, consider Section 1(b) intent to use. Actual qualifying use and evidence are required before registration. Do not use mockups as proof of actual sales/use.
5. Assess each slogan and the logo separately; a GOOOL word-mark application does not separately register them.

Current base government fees: $350 per mark/class, plus $150 per class for an intent-to-use allegation of use. Thus one qualifying single-class application has a $500 baseline through allegation of use, excluding extensions, surcharges, counsel and maintenance. This is not a quote or approval guarantee.
